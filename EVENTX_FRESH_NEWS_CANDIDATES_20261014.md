# Fresh-market news candidates — 14 October 2026

**A larger development candidate pool exists over the same July dates, but outside the original held-market group.** The fixed search found 32 distinct articles overlapping 174 fresh market decisions: 49 movement labels and 125 non-movement labels. These are candidates for a separately designed event study, not a replacement evaluation or a demonstrated news gain.

## What changed in this search

The earlier news checks used original out-of-fold reporting keys. This inventory compares those keys with all existing opened market groups over the same dates: **11 July through 21 July 2026**. The original market groups and scores are preserved.

An event must be available within the preceding 30 minutes of a decision, with **price age at most five minutes** and **at least three trades in the preceding hour**, in addition to inherited example eligibility. These added market-observability requirements are stricter than the original sample. They use only past information.

The text screen reuses the fixed unsigned Fed policy-context rule and separately considers Bitcoin/Ethereum headlines with the same asset in the contract, a reporting/event cue and no modal/forecast or prediction-market-reference wording. No crypto candidate survives all the later-date/fresh-market screens. All retained articles come from the Fed context branch.

The complete candidate ledger and review packet were hashed **before reading movement labels**. Candidate ordering prioritizes original-fold coverage and low publication lag, without selecting on future movements. The resulting class counts are descriptive checks, not article-selection criteria.

## How to use this result

There is a feasible opened-development pilot with both outcome classes when the cohort includes all active market groups. Its role is to help define an event cohort and diagnose sample concentration. It cannot confirm the selected market model or news content, and should not be presented as the original fold-5 test.

Next independently review the 32 articles, group repeated coverage into verified CPI/PPI/testimony or other episodes, and confirm historical market predicate/status and event timing. Then specify a new event-based forward split and obtain faster source arrivals for future evaluation. The source delay and five-market concentration still limit scientific claims.
