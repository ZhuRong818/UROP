# EventX market–news research: plan and progress

**Reporting date:** updated 9 October 2026, Asia/Singapore  
**Scope:** Prediction of 30-minute absolute canonical-YES price repricing from executed-market history and source-available news. KOL and social-virality research are deferred.  
**Controlling records:** [Complete research plan](COMPLETE_RESEARCH_PLAN.md), [current handoff](CURRENT_STATE.md), frozen protocols and results cited below. This report summarizes them; it does not amend a protocol.

## 1. Research question and present answer

The central market–news question is whether news **available to the research system at a decision time** improves a forecast of the next 30-minute absolute prediction-market price movement beyond what executed trades already reveal. It is a predictive comparison, not a causal estimate of an article's effect, a forecast of final contract resolution, or a directional trading strategy. The project uses only Lumid FinData for observational source data and reports missing or uncertified feed intervals as unknown rather than as zero news. [Research plan](COMPLETE_RESEARCH_PLAN.md) · [V3.1 protocol](eventx/release/v3_1_historical_primary/protocol.json)

The strongest **completed** result is the frozen V3.1 historical development comparison. Adding three rolling counts of conservatively available, lexically associated news articles to a market-history logistic model worsened both average precision and Brier score on matched out-of-fold rows. It failed every prespecified promotion condition, so only the market-history model was evaluated on the historical holdout. That holdout supplies an absolute market-only benchmark, **not** a held-out estimate of news lift. [Selection result](data/v3_1_historical/primary/development/selection_result.json) · [Holdout result](data/v3_1_historical/primary/holdout/evaluation_result.json)

A separate V4 prospective test of richer news features is hash-frozen and collecting through 30 October 2026. Its outcome has not been evaluated. V5 and V6 investigate stronger market baselines and contract-aware news linking on previously opened development data; neither has a source-certified, independently validated news exposure frame or a new predictive confirmation result. [V4 protocol](eventx/release/v4/protocol.json) · [V6 readiness review](eventx/release/v6/PLAN_DATA_ALGORITHM_RUBRIC_EVALUATION_20260927.md)

## 3. Data, target and evaluation method

### 3.1 Historical V3.1 cohort and measurement

The corrected, label-blind historical cohort has **4,651 markets**: 3,832 Kalshi and 819 Polymarket. Development is `[2026-05-17, 2026-07-22)` UTC; the consumed historical holdout is `[2026-07-22, 2026-08-08)` UTC. The original V3 activity gate found **204,069 Kalshi rows with zero executed contracts** and, after positive-notional normalization, 19 markets that missed the activity minimum. V3.1 removed exactly those 19 before development labels or outcomes were inspected; the failed V3 gate remains part of the record. The cohort uses a unique canonical YES outcome, at least 100 positive-notional YES executions and at least ten active UTC development days. [V3.1 protocol](eventx/release/v3_1_historical_primary/protocol.json)

At each five-minute UTC decision time, the primary price is the latest executed canonical-YES trade at or before that time, carried forward with its age recorded. Kalshi cents are converted to probabilities; Polymarket uses the unique YES asset. After clipping price to `[0.0001, 0.9999]`, the target asks whether the **absolute** log-odds change from `t` to `t+30 minutes` exceeds the frozen trailing-volatility heuristic `4 × sigma_1m,240`. This is not “four 30-minute Gaussian standard deviations.” The eligibility rule requires trailing activity/history and a full pre-terminal forecast horizon. Candles and midpoints do not replace executed-trade prices in the primary study. [V3.1 protocol](eventx/release/v3_1_historical_primary/protocol.json)

News identity is normalized URL, falling back to article ID, counted once at the earliest conservative availability time. That time is the maximum of publication, ingestion and joined source-run start/end times when present. Missing or conflicting provenance and lags over 24 hours are excluded from the primary V3.1 news features. This clock is conservative; it is **not** a measured first-client-delivery timestamp or a certificate that every upstream page arrived. The frozen development association inventory has **147,377 documents** and **218,496 article–market associations**; these are lexical candidates, not fully audited contract-specific semantic links. [V3.1 protocol](eventx/release/v3_1_historical_primary/protocol.json)

### 3.2 Validation and integrity

V3.1 used five purged, expanding-time folds, each scored on a disjoint market-hash group and an 11-day calendar window. B0 and B1 used identical validation keys. Training-only standardization and a 30-minute horizon purge protect the comparison. A 5,000-replicate paired moving-block bootstrap within markets supplied a conditional development interval. The five promotion conditions required positive aggregate AP and Brier changes, both positive in at least four folds, a positive Brier interval lower bound, and nonnegative Brier change in each venue. The one-shot fallback was specified before the historical holdout. [V3.1 protocol](eventx/release/v3_1_historical_primary/protocol.json) · [Selection result](data/v3_1_historical/primary/development/selection_result.json)

The historical holdout was evaluated once and is consumed. Its builder exposed **26,381 positive labels and prevalence 0.124604 before the consumption receipt**, contrary to the intended receipt order. Candidate weights, transforms and evaluation formulas were already frozen, and no prediction or performance metric had been read. The original output and truthfulness-only amendment are preserved. This remains a real protocol deviation in the holdout claim. [Deviation record](eventx/release/v3_1_historical_primary/HOLDOUT_PROTOCOL_DEVIATION.md) · [Consumption receipt](data/v3_1_historical/primary/holdout/holdout_consumption_receipt.json)

## 4. Methods and algorithms tried

### 4.1 Frozen V3.1 B0/B1 comparison

**B0** is a 12-feature standardized logistic SGD ensemble: current price log odds; 5-, 30- and 120-minute momentum; 30- and 240-minute realized volatility; 60- and 240-minute trade counts and notional; minutes since the last trade; and venue. **B1** uses the same learner and rows, adding article counts over 60, 360 and 1,440 minutes plus a schema-retained financial-symbol indicator that was constant zero in this study. Count/notional inputs use `log1p`. Both candidates use fixed seeds 11, 23 and 47, eight epochs, L2 coefficient 0.0001 and learning rate 0.02; there was no hyperparameter search for this comparison. This tests a **count-based representation of the frozen association rule**, not every way of using news text. [V3.1 protocol](eventx/release/v3_1_historical_primary/protocol.json)

### 4.2 Frozen V4 nested news ladder

V4 retained the market-history reference and froze **B1** news volume, **B2** relevance plus seven-day market-relative novelty, and **B3** publisher diversity plus exact-headline cross-publisher diffusion. The primary prospective contrast is B3 against B0 in 470 Polymarket markets. Success requires at least **0.001 nats per row** log-loss improvement, a paired bootstrap lower bound above zero, a Brier deterioration no worse than 0.0005, and the venue guardrail. Its semantic transform, three-seed model bundles and post-collection pipeline were frozen before the future interval; the pre-holdout verifier passed 29 checks. V4 measures cross-publisher repetition, **not** social shares, views or virality. Kalshi was excluded from this V4 cohort after source reconciliation failed. [V4 protocol](eventx/release/v4/protocol.json) · [Pre-holdout verification](data/v4/confirmatory/pre_holdout_implementation_verification.json)

The already opened V4 **historical discovery** run compared the ladder on 745,443 matched rows. Its semantic vocabulary/SVD had been fitted on the complete historical discovery corpus before individual folds, an unsupervised temporal preprocessing limitation. The future V4 representation was fixed before its prospective interval, so this historical limitation must be disclosed without substituting the discovery result for the pending confirmation. [Discovery result](data/v4/discovery/models/result.json) · [Algorithm audit](eventx/release/v5/ALGORITHM_AUDIT_20260907.md)

### 4.3 V5 baseline and news attribution experiments

Opened-history V5 diagnostics first found that V4's harm was concentrated in news-exposed rows and in large negative corrections; the no-news majority changed little. A gated residual-news architecture was then explored. A converged nonlinear residual initially appeared to improve a linear market baseline, but **matched controls explained the gain more convincingly**: a context-only gate improved more than the full news correction, and a nonlinear market-only model outperformed the full gated-news model. In the fixed comparison, adding the full news mechanism to a gated context control changed log loss by **−0.000871** (95% descriptive interval `[−0.001457, −0.000208]`), while the global nonlinear market model beat the full news model by **0.008543**. These results are opened-data diagnostics, not a prospective V5 result. [Research plan, V5 direction](COMPLETE_RESEARCH_PLAN.md)

An explicit algorithm screen used the **same 364,903 opened validation rows across 1,135 contracts** and capped training/tuning samples for matched L2 logistic, quadratic quantile-spline logistic and shallow histogram boosted-tree market models. Market-only log loss was **0.448075**, **0.423706** and **0.405075**, respectively. A 9 October paired reanalysis of the frozen *no-terminal* predictions found tree versus linear log loss **0.407391 versus 0.451070**, an improvement of **0.043679 nats/row (9.68%)**, positive in each of three forward folds; the descriptive market-cluster interval is `[0.039575, 0.047682]`. This makes the market-only baseline gain less dependent on the terminal predictor, while leaving upstream as-of questions open. Adding news timing or relevance–novelty did not yield a stable gain over matched controls; for trees, timing and semantic variants made identical predictions on the evaluation rows. The tree result is an exploratory baseline screen, not an automatic replacement for the selected V5 specification or evidence of news lift. [Algorithm audit](eventx/release/v5/ALGORITHM_AUDIT_20260907.md) · [Paired diagnostic](eventx/release/v5/BASELINE_IMPROVEMENT_AND_NEWS_GATE_20261009.md)

At the user's request, a separate 9 October exploratory fixed-offset run scored **9,965 opened rows with an observed recent article** using the frozen no-terminal tree baseline. An exposure-gated context correction improved log loss from **0.330591 to 0.327533**. Adding news count/age worsened it to **0.327942**; adding relevance–novelty worsened it further to **0.328787**. The full news arm was **0.001254 nats/row worse than the matched context correction**. This is a numerical forecast comparison before V6 source certification, but it is not an independent news-lift result: source versions and historical semantic timing remain unverified, and no missing-feed interval was coded as zero news. [Exploratory trial](eventx/release/v5/OPENED_NEWS_OFFSET_TRIAL_20261009.md) · [Exact result](data/v5/opened_news_offset_trial_20261009/report.json)

A broader 9 October opened-data search added **50 configurations** across fixed-offset, spline, boosted-tree, bagged-tree, MLP and CatBoost methods. The fold-4-selected market-only histogram booster improved fold-5 log loss from **0.348884 to 0.344816** on 112,616 identical rows, with Brier and AP gains. On the exact 5,388 fold-5 observed-article rows, it scored **0.243727** versus **0.244576** for the fold-4-selected news model. CatBoost's lower post-hoc full-row fold-5 market score was not the fold-4 winner, and LightGBM/XGBoost could not load without the missing macOS OpenMP runtime. These adaptive, outcome-open results support a stronger *market-only* baseline but still do not show stable incremental news value. [Full method survey](eventx/release/v5/OPENED_METHOD_SURVEY_20261009.md) · [Exact same-row comparison](data/v5/method_screen_observed_row_join_20261009/report.json)

A separate direct-versus-stacked market-only comparison selected the **direct nonlinear B0-v5N** development candidate: log loss **0.428513**, Brier **0.135783**, AP **0.278015** on 364,903 sequential out-of-fold rows. It beat the stacked nonlinear variant by **0.003611** log loss (paired interval `[0.001359, 0.005694]`). Its fitting and sample design differ from the capped tree screen, so these absolute scores should not be ranked against one another as a controlled architecture contest. B0-v5N is still **unfrozen and unconfirmed**. [Research plan, V5 direction](COMPLETE_RESEARCH_PLAN.md) · [V5 protocol revision](eventx/release/v5/PROTOCOL_V1_1.md)

The V5-R proposal would make a news correction only when a valid recent article is present, train that correction against an honestly cross-fitted market baseline, and compare it with equal-gated context and timing controls. A true fixed-baseline offset, as-of metadata, within-fold semantic fitting, nested purges, approved event families and a dependence method must be implemented and frozen before any new labels. Earlier residual fits optimized a standalone expert/blend rather than the proposed penalized offset, and their terminal metadata were not joined as of each decision time. These are reasons to treat V5 as a design cycle, not a completed news test. [V5 protocol revision](eventx/release/v5/PROTOCOL_V1_1.md) · [Algorithm audit](eventx/release/v5/ALGORITHM_AUDIT_20260907.md)

### 4.4 V6 contract-aware linking and semantic trials

V6 tackles a measurement problem left by lexical article counts: a story about an asset or event may be topical but may not apply to a contract's exact entity, predicate, threshold, date, side or settlement rule. The first release built **4,651 contract cards**, including subtitles and separate combo/leg evidence, and generated BM25 article–card candidates. Later development compared lexical TF-IDF (**L0**), field-aware typed checks with question/subtitle ranking (**L1**), and a pinned cross-encoder reranking the same typed candidate union (**L2**), with dense retrieval as an additional candidate source. These are candidate-generation and ranking components with abstention; they are not an admitted forecasting feature. [Implementation status](eventx/release/v6/IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS_20260916.md) · [Support and pipeline report](eventx/release/v6/SUPPORT_AND_PIPELINE_READINESS_20260926.md)

On a fixed opened set of **70 articles and 134 contract cards**, the common candidate union had **669 pairs**. Dense retrieval added 201 pairs and 11 typed hypotheses but **no new article with a typed hypothesis**; the 52 typed hypotheses came from only eight articles, seven Bitcoin-related. L2 changed five article top sets relative to L1. Without independent reference labels for this revised context endpoint, none of these counts establishes precision, recall or a better linker. [Support and pipeline report](eventx/release/v6/SUPPORT_AND_PIPELINE_READINESS_20260926.md)

The older, stricter bounded audit required direct article–contract YES-proposition evidence. It found **39/40 conservative relevance errors** on its fixed top-choice frame, including two unresolved reviewer disagreements counted as errors; the quality gate failed. Family separation was inconclusive (3/74 errors), while story separation passed within its sampled frame (0/72). The old cycle is consumed and cannot be relabeled into a success for a broader context definition. A later **102-pair relaxed development review** yielded 26 material-predicate, 21 shared-context and 55 not-applicable labels; its 26 material labels reuse only five articles. Thirty-nine pairs required one assistant adjudication after two user-attested human reviews. This is a sensitivity/error-taxonomy exercise, not independent gold for a frozen new endpoint. [Bounded audit](eventx/release/v6/BOUNDED_AUDIT_RESULT_20260917.md) · [Relaxed adjudication](eventx/release/v6/RELAXED_ADJUDICATION_AND_NEXT_STEP_20260928.md)

Several semantic component trials also failed promotion: a bounded NLI probe falsely selected 3/10 unsupported synthetic cases; a pinned local structured-span model produced **0/10 schema-valid outputs**; parser/compositional trials found actor, speaker, negation and attachment errors. These probes help identify failure modes but are neither representative field accuracy nor a safe automatic link gate. [NLI trial](eventx/release/v6/NLI_BINDING_TRIAL_20260925.md) · [Span trial](eventx/release/v6/SPAN_EXTRACTION_TRIAL_20260925.md) · [V6 method review](eventx/release/v6/PLAN_DATA_ALGORITHM_RUBRIC_EVALUATION_20260927.md)
