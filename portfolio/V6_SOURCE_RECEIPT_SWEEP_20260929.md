The only relevant candidates they found were `provenance.scrape_cursor` (latest-state overwrite) and `lumid_data_meta.audit_log` (API-surface operations, not loader pages or symbols). This table-name/role-visible sweep is strong evidence about the accessible database, but not proof that file logs, hidden tables, JSON fields or another deployment retain nothing. The remaining source-owner question is whether `/data/findata/loaders/run.py` and the Finnhub collector wrote **retained file logs** with the exact request bounds, symbol roster, page attempts/commits/failures and cursor transitions for the nine scoped article runs.

The transition time and early count are user-relayed; without a symbol roster, neither dropped-membership claim is verifiable from count alone. The researcher cannot date the latter change on their 7 June frozen replica. FMP date bounds identify requested ranges, not completed pages. Neither family gains a full-window certificate from run arguments alone.
