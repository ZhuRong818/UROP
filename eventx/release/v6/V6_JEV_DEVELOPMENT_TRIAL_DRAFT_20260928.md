## Why and where

Jev can answer narrow typed questions about one article–contract pair. Try it as a **semantic classification component** after candidate retrieval: relation (`same_event_update`, `shared_mechanism_context`, `generic_topic_only`, `incompatible`, `unknown`) and applicability (`direct_proposition`, `shared_context_only`, `not_applicable`, `unknown`). Keep each question independent. It does not create source coverage, article availability, market tradability, settlement evidence or a 30-minute forecast. TypeSafe's [model list](https://docs.typesafe.ai/models) currently identifies `jev-1.13.0`; pin that ID rather than the moving alias. The [official API](https://docs.typesafe.ai/api) returns typed answers. Its [documented limitations](https://docs.typesafe.ai/model-jaggedness/jev-1.13) include date comparison, numeric precision, indirection and long irrelevant state, so code keeps time, rules arithmetic and eligibility gates.

## Proposed two-question schema

One Choice asks: “Based only on the supplied article and contract text, which relationship is supported by exact stated subject/event and mechanism? Choose unknown when required identity, episode or text is missing.” Options are the five relation labels above, with the review-guide definitions, including team-versus-host and company-versus-asset exclusions. A separate Choice asks: “For this precise contract proposition, is the article direct evidence, shared context with an explicit mechanism, inapplicable, or unknown?” The criteria make strike, stage, period and metric requirements explicit. Do not ask Jev to infer the observed YES/NO resolution or to do date arithmetic. Claim role and exact evidence spans stay human/structured-extractor fields for this initial diagnostic, not generated explanations.

The relation and applicability outputs remain separate. Compare them with the two corresponding opened relaxed fields and the five-bucket [class map](V6_RELAXED_CLASS_MAP_DRAFT_20260928.md). In particular, inspect all 16 `material_predicate_evidence` + `shared_mechanism_context` cases separately; a broad context judgment is not a strict direct match. A Jev answer cannot convert the assistant-adjudicated labels into independent human gold.
