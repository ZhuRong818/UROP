# V6 relaxed review class map — opened-development draft

Date: 28 September 2026. This map is specified **after** the 102 relaxed labels were opened. It is a transparent development interpretation for the next method version, not prospective validation of those labels, original-rubric gold, an independent audit, or a change to the existing primary endpoint. Original reviewer files, adjudication and frozen artifacts remain unchanged.

## Separate judgments

The semantic relation, contract applicability, claim role, settlement evidence, source eligibility and predictive exposure must remain separate. A material topic can affect a forecast without proving YES or NO. A direct claim can still be ineligible if its article or contract version was unavailable or the market was not tradable. `directional_evidence` is a reviewer's interpretation of tone/direction, not the observed resolution; `retrospective_development_use` is not a point-in-time source certificate.

The original fixed strict direct/proposition rubric remains the planned primary link reference. These relaxed labels can diagnose a future revised context endpoint only after that endpoint is explicitly versioned and independently reviewed. Do not transform them into original-rubric answers.

## Proposed future relevance endpoint, to freeze before a new audit

Two positive classes must be reported separately: (1) `event_update`, where quoted text identifies the same event/episode and provides a relevant update or observation; and (2) `mechanism_context`, where quoted text states an action, measurement, report or opinion with an explicit permitted mechanism for the contract subject. The latter is predictive context, **not** direct proposition or settlement evidence. `generic_topic_only`, `incompatible` and verified wrong entity/stage/period are negatives. Missing article/contract text, unclear mechanism, unresolved identity or conflicting reviewers remain abstentions. An article may link to several contracts, but it counts once as a story exposure; applicability is assessed separately for each contract. Multi-leg contracts require evidence for the relevant underlying leg and mapping; a broad parent-topic match is insufficient.

For the revised endpoint, a later protocol must choose whether `mechanism_context` joins `event_update` in its primary positive class or is secondary. Until that choice and a fresh review are frozen, report both classes separately and retain the original strict reference as primary. Opinion/forecast can be context if the cited text explicitly states its subject and mechanism; it cannot be retyped as an observed action. A same-series price observation needs the exact asset, period and metric before it can be an event update. YES/NO direction and settlement evidence remain separate fields.
