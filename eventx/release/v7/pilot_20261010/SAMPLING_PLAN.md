# U.S. macro news evidence pilot

Date: 10 October 2026. Authorized by the user's instruction to execute the next research steps. This fixes a development packet, not a forecasting freeze or an independent held-out linker audit.

## Population and budget

Use only the already opened 17 May–22 July historical article corpus and the cached V6 contract cards. Select single-contract cards mentioning Federal Reserve, Fed, FOMC, CPI, PCE, nonfarm/payroll, unemployment or GDP; exclude explicit foreign-country/central-bank scopes. Keyword membership is a proposed domain route, not verified semantic applicability. No price, forecast label, old reviewer answer or subsequent market reaction determines selection.

Exclude the original 200-article pilot, the 500-article expansion and the 100 reserved article IDs/content hashes. Reserved records are projected to identifiers/hashes solely for exclusion. Exact repeated content is reduced to its earliest conservative availability; retain the multiplicity and original source identity. Syndication and nonexact story duplication remain human review tasks.

Route headline/summary to monetary policy, inflation or employment using the fixed patterns in the builder. Construct availability-date × route buckets. Uniformly sample 30 buckets without replacement, then one article uniformly within each selected bucket, using seed 20261010. These are sampling buckets, **not 30 independent information episodes**. Date/route grouping deliberately increases diversity and changes article inclusion probabilities.

For each article, retrieve BM25 top 20 over the domain cards. Draw four disjoint pair strata: top-one census; one uniform rank 2–10 alternative; one uniform rank 11–20 rejected by the top-one retrieval policy; and one uniform pair outside top 20. Alternatives are hard-negative candidates, not labeled negatives. Stop if the target packet cannot be drawn; retain shortage evidence rather than silently widening scope. Save each frame size and article/pair inclusion probability before labels. Scores, ranks, sampling strata and previous judgments are absent from the semantic packet.

Budget: 120 pairs, two independent reviewers (240 judgments), at most one adjudication for each disagreement (120 additional judgments maximum). No resampling after labels to improve apparent accuracy. This packet is development-only; later algorithm evaluation requires a separate episode-disjoint sample and stable linker.

## Review inputs and yield

Review exact headline/summary bytes, exact question/subtitle/rule bytes when present, and at most three prior available reports from the same broad route within seven days. Prior reporting is bounded context, not proof of all previously public information. Keep source/version/status evidence in a separate operations sheet. Missing rules remain missing; current or retrospective metadata cannot certify historical availability.

Report frame counts, date/route coverage, contract/event counts, actual independently adjudicated episode count, role-specific agreement, uncertainty, material-link coverage and evidenced outside-retrieval misses. Inverse-probability estimates apply only to the declared finite routed frame and need episode-aware uncertainty. The enriched packet's raw accuracy is not production accuracy or global recall. No semantic accuracy, novelty, predictive improvement or independent episode count exists before reviewer responses.

## Next gate

Two outcome-blind reviewers record independence and prior exposure, return unchanged-ID responses, then one documented adjudication resolves or retains unknowns. Episode identities and duplicate lineage must be reviewed before assigning training/evaluation splits. Human-supplied explanatory fields remain separate from machine features until an automated, decision-time implementation exists. Labels from future evaluation events must never become prediction-time features.
