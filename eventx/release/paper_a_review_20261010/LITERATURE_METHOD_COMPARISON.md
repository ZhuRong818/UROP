# Focused method comparison for Paper A and V7

Checked 10 October 2026. This extends the earlier abstract-level positioning with selected full-text methods. It is not an exhaustive systematic review or independent replication.

| Primary source | Method inspected | Distinction relevant to EventX |
|---|---|---|
| [Angelini and De Angelis, arXiv 2606.07811v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.07811v1) | One-minute Kalshi NBA quote midpoints merged with timestamped play-by-play. Their public-state benchmark uses game-level cross-fitting and excludes contemporaneous live prices; they examine updating gaps, later drift and spreads/activity. | Structured game-state evidence supplies a value benchmark. EventX currently uses delayed textual evidence and forecasts unsigned repricing; it lacks an independent fundamental-probability benchmark for a causal or efficient-updating claim. Cross-fitting by game is also different from forward chronological validation. |
| [Ibrahim and Zaki, arXiv 2609.06005v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.06005v1) | Sentence-to-market retrieval and human-blinded validation distinguish odds citations from correct attribution. The paper separates a machine-positive precision audit from a small developmental recall diagnostic and discloses full-corpus measurement development before temporal coupling evaluation. | EventX should track external versus market-derived spans, audit retrieval misses separately and disclose measurement chronology. Their temporal result does not establish an untouched evaluation of the measurement pipeline or causal price-to-news feedback. |
| [Gong and Dimitrov, SSRN 7005158](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7005158) | The page remained inaccessible in this check. Earlier positioning is limited to the indexed abstract concerning daily text and tail-risk decisions; the author's profile confirms the working-paper title. | No full-method assessment is claimed. Obtain the paper before asserting detailed methodological novelty or comparing its evaluation to EventX. [Author's record](https://github.com/garroshub) |
| [Giacomini and White, Econometrica 2006](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1468-0262.2006.00718.x) | Forecast-method comparison distinguishes conditional from unconditional predictive ability and accounts for estimation procedures. | A conditional heterogeneity question and a population-average gain need different stated nulls and assumptions. A named test cannot substitute for a design-specific dependence assessment. |
| [Zhu and Timmermann, arXiv 2006.03238v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2006.03238) | The paper discusses restrictive conditions behind conditional equal-accuracy hypotheses and serial dependence in loss differences, including practical inference consequences. | Neither a generic HAC estimator nor a conditional-test label guarantees valid inference for EventX's overlapping events, adaptive models and calendar shocks. This motivates checking the declared estimand and dependence assumptions. |

Before submission, expand the search to prior textual prediction-market forecasting, event-time microstructure, point-in-time retrieval and evidence-grounding benchmarks. Keep exact source versions and access limits. The recent working papers are provisional research records, not established consensus.
